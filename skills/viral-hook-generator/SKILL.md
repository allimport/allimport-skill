# Viral Hook Generator

## When to use
- Writing a short-form video script, social post, or ad opener
- Existing content has good substance but weak engagement/retention at the start

## Process
1. **Identify the core tension** — the surprising fact, contradiction, or stakes that makes someone want to know more.
2. **Pick a hook pattern**: bold claim, pattern interrupt ("everyone says X, but..."), specific number/result, direct callout of the viewer's problem, or open loop ("the reason X failed isn't what you think").
3. **Front-load the payoff signal** — the hook should promise a specific outcome, not just create vague curiosity.
4. **Cut throat-clearing** — no "hey guys", no slow setup; the hook IS the first line.
5. **Generate 3-5 variants** and pick based on specificity and stakes, not cleverness alone.

## Checklist
- [ ] Hook delivers a concrete promise, not vague intrigue
- [ ] No wasted words before the hook lands
- [ ] Hook matches what the content actually delivers (no bait without payoff)
