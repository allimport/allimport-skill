# Ad Copy Generator

## When to use
- Writing copy for paid ads (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn)
- Existing ad copy has low CTR or conversion rate and needs a rewrite

## Process
1. **Nail the offer and audience** — who exactly this is for, and what specific outcome they get.
2. **Lead with the benefit, not the feature** — what changes for the customer, not what the product does technically.
3. **Match copy to funnel stage** — cold audience needs problem-awareness framing; retargeting can reference the product directly.
4. **Write a clear, single CTA** — one action, stated directly ("Start free trial", not "Learn more about how we can help").
5. **Generate variants for testing** — vary the hook/angle (pain point vs aspiration vs social proof), not just synonyms of the same line.

## Checklist
- [ ] Copy states a specific benefit, not a vague claim
- [ ] One clear CTA per ad
- [ ] Variants differ by angle, not just wording
- [ ] Matches platform constraints (character limits, tone norms)
