# Pricing Psychology

## When to use
- Setting up or revising pricing tiers/plans
- Conversion is low and price presentation may be the cause

## Process
1. **Anchor with a clear reference point** — show the highest-value tier first or alongside others so mid-tier looks like the obvious choice.
2. **Limit choice to 3 tiers** when possible — too many options causes decision paralysis; too few removes anchoring.
3. **Make the recommended plan visually obvious** ("Most popular") and price it as the rational middle choice.
4. **Frame price relative to value delivered**, not just cost — "$X/month" vs "less than a coffee a day" only when it's actually a meaningful reframe, not filler.
5. **Be honest about what's gated** — psychology tactics (anchoring, framing) are fine; hiding real limitations or fees is not.

## Checklist
- [ ] Tiers structured to guide toward one clear recommended choice
- [ ] Price framing matches actual value delivered, not exaggerated
- [ ] No hidden fees or misleading anchors
